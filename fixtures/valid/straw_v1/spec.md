## 1. Executive Summary & Design Rationale
Resip™ is a solid-state, battery-free thermal exchange drink straw engineered for backcountry hikers, ultra-light campers, and cold-weather outdoor enthusiasts. By eliminating microcontrollers, servos, batteries, and luxury fluff down to raw physical engineering, Resip™ delivers instant, sip-by-sip beverage reheating (5°C stream $\rightarrow$ 18–22°C drinkable warmth) using only mechanical phase-change enthalpy.

## 2. Core Operating Principles
- **Phase-Change Exothermic Core**: 50g of sealed supersaturated Sodium Acetate Trihydrate ($CH_3COONa \cdot 3H_2O$) capable of releasing $12.05\text{ kJ}$ latent heat of fusion at a fixed $54.0^\circ\text{C}$ ($129.2^\circ\text{F}$) plateau.
- **Bistable Nucleation Trigger**: 301 full-hard stainless steel mechanical snap-disc embedded in the mid-body. A single manual click triggers rapid crystallization across the chamber without electrical power.
- **Food-Grade 316L Stainless Conduit**: Central $6.0\text{mm}$ ID $\times 0.5\text{mm}$ wall tube maximizing heat transfer coefficient ($R_{wall} = 0.45\text{ K/W}$) to the moving liquid stream.
- **Thermochromic Silicone Jacket**: Rugged 1.5mm silicone outer insulation sleeve ($R_{env} = 2.20\text{ K/W}$) with thermochromic pigment that transitions color when the straw is activated and hot.

## 3. Backcountry Reset & Maintenance
- **Field Reset**: Submerge straw in boiling water over any camp stove (Jetboil, MSR, campfire kettle) for 8–10 minutes until internal crystal matrix is fully liquefied.
- **Shelf Stability**: Indefinitely supercoolable at sub-freezing ambient temperatures until mechanical trigger click.
- **Zero Electronics / Zero Batteries**: 100% waterproof, drop-proof, and freeze-tolerant.
