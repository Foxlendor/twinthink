# Connected TwinThink experience

## Authority and preservation

The revised user brief received 2 October 2026 rejects the handcrafted pipe experiment as the
product direction. It is preserved in a named Git stash on `fall-v0`, not published. Work now
starts from production `main` at `51226866bebf7b9c07f3b4b72fb266f48a51688a`.

Preserve the live Slate's exact renderer, movement physics, typography, day/night palette,
composition, controls, media, authorship and shared exploration. Impact is only a movement
appearance reference. There is no fixed two-choice rule or themed educational environment.
The existing `fall-space` branch is separate prior work, not a replacement for this baseline.

## Connected product requirements

Expression and sharing: existing private-first Shadows, media, build-on, forks, invitations,
sharing and moderation remain the creation system. People can attach a relationship and write
why it matters without a restrictive taxonomy. Authorship of the connection is separate from
authorship of either endpoint. A visitor association never becomes a creator's genealogy.
AI-assisted drafts remain clearly labeled and require an explicit save/share action.

Traversal: accessible explicit connections use the existing spatial movement and openings.
Recorded parentage remains evidence; inferred word matches are labeled as associations rather
than influence. Deliberate wandering is an ordinary option, not disguised as documented lineage.

Continuity and recap: retain bounded device-local journeys, their actual offered alternatives,
and existing return paths. Revalidate source and destination visibility when showing a recap
and again when following it. A question may invite exploration of one actual alternative using
its real explanation, without disclosing a private path or inventing a mystery. A person can
inspect the explanation, ask deeper questions, follow, or ignore it. No resonance score.

Conversation: optional and entered from reflection, not mandatory at entry or intrusive during
the Fall. Separate evidence, association and speculation. An analogy only imports the named
aspect; suggestions are not adopted decisions. AI can challenge unsupported conclusions and
help form an idea for review, but cannot publish or edit canon. External processing requires
explicit consent for the selected accessible excerpts and conversation; never upload a Trail.
Voice records only after a person's action, shows the transcript before sending it as a question,
and offers disclosed synthetic speech. Missing provider credentials mean unavailable, not fake AI.

Shared exploration: existing per-viewer access and leader/follower agency remain authoritative.
Following a relationship uses the same destination movement as the rest of the Slate, so shared
location handling continues to apply. No central collection of Leans or private journeys.

## Canon and open decisions retained

Slate, Shadow Slate, Shadow, Drop/Fall/Drift, Lean, Whoeuvre, private Trail/Your Fall, explicit
Dew/Drop and ambient resonance remain distinct. Rabi remains architect, not recommender.
Current, reservoirs, allocation, overflow, desire paths, co-op, capacity, founders, provenance,
controlled disclosure, paper/share, physical Twin Pen and separate Lean research remain in the
destination. Unsettled economics, aggregate navigation privacy, research claims and hardware
protocols are not silently implemented or deleted. See CORE_LEDGER and TWINTHINK_CORE_SPEC.

## Engineering boundaries and evidence

Connections have durable ids, provenance, explanation, optional author-written reflective question,
visibility and timestamps. Device-local records are never silently promoted to the server.
Published connections require accessible public endpoints; hiding either endpoint hides the
connection and its explanation. A contributor may retract their own connection, not someone else's.

Implementation, tests, browser preview, deployment and experience acceptance are separate states.
No release is claimed until its exact commit and deployment are verified. A local preview can
exercise creation and connections without cloud credentials; provider-backed conversation,
transcription and speech must additionally be tested with configured services. This document
tracks the whole connected objective; it is not a substitute for implementation.
