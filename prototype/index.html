          How to move

          Let go
          The Drop keeps falling. Gaps let it through; strands catch it.
          Drift
          Drag with your thumb (or mouse) from anywhere on the web: direction and strength nudge its course. Keys: arrows or WASD.
          Touch or hold
          Touch without dragging: the Drop slows and settles beside the nearest work so you can read it. Key: hold Space.
          Release
          Lift your thumb: motion resumes.
          Dew
          A decision to carry this forward: the Drop adheres and leaves a bead on the work (on this device). Key: E.
          Drop
          A decision to let go: it detaches and falls. Where it lands is up to the web. Key: Q.

          Being carried somewhere, settling, or reading records nothing. Only Dew and Drop are kept, on this device. The phone’s tilt is never used.
